# Hull House neighborhood block art proposal

The owner requests one Hull-House neighborhood block built from period maps and photographs, with a JPG preview from the artist. The setting remains Chicago around 1893, at night, with warm browns, native animal scale and the accepted festive banners and ribbons. This is the next art assignment after the covered stockyard pen and custom gate reach their first JPG checkpoint.

The architect selects the block bounded by South Halsted, South Desplaines, West Polk and Ewing Streets, immediately east of Hull House. These boundaries are visible on Nationalities Map No. 1 in the 1895 publication. The assignment covers the block's street fronts, buildings, passages and rear spaces. The settlement across Halsted supplies historical context and photo reference.

Establish physical walls, building heights, roof forms and construction materials from compatible insurance sheets and dated photographs.

Find photographs of the selected street fronts where available. The circa 1891 Hull-House entrance is a dated nearby lead. Use dated neighborhood photographs for comparable facade and yard details when the particular property is unpictured. Prefer printed dimensions and a readable original scale. Record calibration endpoints and uncertainty for a derived measurement. The educational map reproduction inspected by the architect establishes location and outline only. Preserve proportions when placing native modules. List any game-grid adjustment, substituted roof, inferred storey count or invented opening alongside its source. Later settlement additions and later streetscapes require their actual dates in the evidence record.

Preserve meaningful open yards and routes. Repeated buildings should retain their actual frontage and corner treatment. Use local repair details, work fittings, domestic objects and signs to make occupied spaces legible where the references support them.

Use the existing native wall, door, floor and roof systems, the pinned Farmyard and Nautilus components, and current material slots as the starting kit. Keep the 1 by 2 by .16 m wall, 1 by 1.65 by .12 m door, .12 m slab and 2.12 m floor pitch. New forms should address a specific documented gap in that kit. The owner wants the fair's jubilation to appear through the city. Add a restrained festive cloth skin to selected street-facing shops or other suitable attachment points, with weathered banners and ribbons readable at normal zoom. Deliver these JPGs at least 1280 by 800 pixels.
