## What we want to learn

| Interaction | Useful observation |
| --- | --- |
| Exploration and objects | Can mouse/joystick and keyboard users reach doors, carry light, traverse stairs and return without a crash, trapped pawn or duplicated object? |
| Different fire starts | How do a shop stock/floor, furnished room, exterior timber and a small yard fuel patch behave under the same rules? A fire that burns out is a valid result. |
| Adjacency and distance | What happens at a real party wall, across an air gap and around a corner? Record heat/exposure and paths, rather than demanding that every house catch. |
| Tools, people and clocks | Do facing targets, pouring trails, flame contact, barrels, actor burning and automatic pickups agree with current controls and their stated effects? Include powder-plus-pouring as a short focused case. |
| Witness and resident | Do sight, lantern memory, visible ignition, cover and smoke/exit routes produce explainable reactions as the player improvises? Suspicion alone still does not authorize a shot. |
| Damage and aftermath | Do fire, light, support, falling material, collision and cooling rubble agree as local pieces fail? Per-segment burn age remains authoritative. |
| Cost and lifecycle | Where do input delay, frame stalls, growing resources, reset problems or off-camera errors first become noticeable as more of the block burns? |

These are observation prompts, not win conditions or promises that all behavior already works. Keep a simple matrix with **working, broken, unwired and untested** entries. Catalog presence is not integration evidence. If a tool or pickup lacks an adapter, record that gap and its visible behavior rather than quietly declaring it supported.

- Begin profiling at one burning building, then two and three as short matched observations within the same frozen scene. Record any free-play case beyond that measured envelope. Do not extinguish fires or delete actors silently to stay under a budget.
