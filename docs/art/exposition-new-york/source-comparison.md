# Source measurements and game adaptations

Plate44 of McKim, Mead & White’s monograph; [original plan/elevation](https://archive.org/details/gri_33125006526145/page/n98/mode/1up), [built plate43](https://archive.org/details/gri_33125006526145/page/n96/mode/1up). The original JP2 pages and readable [calibration sheet](../../../art/source/exposition-new-york/annotated-calibration.jpg) preserve the evidence.

The plan uses 6.2 original pixels/foot; elevation uses6.15. Each ruler is calibrated on20–40 ft and cross-checked on10–30 ft. `metres = pixels / calibrationPixels × calibrationFeet × .3048`. The source remains at its measured size. For derived readings the allowance is ±2pixels at each feature endpoint plus2% of the measured span, rounded upward. This is drawing-reading uncertainty, not survey accuracy.

The 154 ft facade is the printed 77 ft + 77 ft chain. Remaining feet values are ruler calculations, not exact readings of the small dimension numerals. The source record retains each pixel span, locator, conversion and adaptation. Main facade grid adjustment: +.0608 m total; native tile size remains1m. Front stair clear flight is 14 m; its 18.3 m envelope includes the cheeks. Turned ornament and fine sculpture are simplified for isometric scale.

The rear footprint follows the complete first-floor plan. Rear opening rhythm, raised-roof depth/plan, rear roof joins and tower rear faces are inferred. Colours, roof covering choice and the exact construction/combustion materials are unresolved. Empty urns simplify the photographed plant displays. Awnings follow the photograph; their colours are an art choice.

## Porch profile and paired columns

The 9.291 m source projection includes its straight neck. The independently measured front-to-back curve diameter is about 16.175 m; the authored curve diameter is 16 m with a 1.25 m connector. Each porch uses 14 outer columns (paired groups) and eight inner columns, preserving the central stair approach. The [source detail](../../../art/source/exposition-new-york/porch-column-source.jpg) and `plan.curvedPorches.columns` record every source centroid and authored position. Authored pairs are mirrored and rounded to .05 m; maximum coordinate adjustment is under .1 m. Column cross-sections and capitals are simplified art forms.
