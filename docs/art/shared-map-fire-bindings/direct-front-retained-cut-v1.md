## Complete non-self-intersection and volume proofs

These constructions do not rely on manifold topology alone:

- Every internal source edge has two opposite uses. Source vertex links are
  interior cycles or boundary paths, with each boundary vertex owned by one loop.
- Each reference has one connected source surface, simple planar boundary loops,
  correctly opposed closure orientation and closed Euler characteristic two.
- **19,991 original triangle-pair tests** prove disjointness or contact confined
  to a shared original vertex/edge. Coplanar positive-area overlap and unowned
  crossings fail.
- **614 source-triangle/implicit-face tests** exclude interior crossings.
  The three baluster disks have strict coordinate separation from one another.
- The complete oriented area-vector sum is exactly zero. The closed surface
  integral is positive and exactly invariant under origin shift (1,2,3) m.

Actual original triangle integrals plus continuous planar-boundary integrals
give volume. No physical cap triangulation or convex hull is needed.
For a reference disk, the contribution is `dot(p, orientedAreaVector)/3`.
It is the explicit closure contribution, not an open integral being treated
as volume.

The rail-box reference independently matches five fully covered retained face
domains plus its recorded boundary side. That establishes equality with the
retained rectangular domain before its axis-plane product is used as a
cross-check. Bounds alone never provide the volume.

- Producer's parsed binary64 value: the exact dyadic stored by that token.
- Captured Float32 coordinate: `5452595/4194304` m,
  or **1.2999999523162841796875 m**.

The actual source fragment's pre-Float32 boundary coordinates and the recorded
producer plane all lie inside the certified rounding cell of that exact

`1/16777216 m` ≈ `5.9604644775e-8 m`. Direct conversion reproduces the original
captured bits. This explains these pinned coordinates; it is not a tolerance
for snapping, welding or adopting unrelated sources.

The signed captured-minus-decimal difference is
`-1/20971520 m` ≈ **-4.7683715820e-8 m**.
The captured-minus-recorded-binary64 value is also retained exactly.
With the retained material on the positive-X side, this negative clearance
places its captured boundary slightly inside the recorded guard gap.

The rail-box's intersection with that recorded cut is explicitly **positive**:
approximately **2.212529291008019e-9 m³**, proved using the retained-domain
cross-check and exact cut intersection. It is not rounded to zero.
For the baluster, the signed clearance is proved; an exact recorded-cut
intersection volume is not asserted.

These reference constructions describe the **captured retained material**.
