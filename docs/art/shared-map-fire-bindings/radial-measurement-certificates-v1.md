## Actual affine volume and area propagation

Each coefficient of the original affine linear matrix L is interpreted as its
stored binary value. Translation stays outside the source-origin integral.
No claim is made that this bounds the author's intended unrounded matrix.

For the original source signed integral V and seam-only error Ev:

- Nominal absolute world integral: `abs(det(L)) * V`.
- World seam-only error: `abs(det(L)) * Ev`.
- World reference interval: `[nominal-error, nominal+error]`.
- Conservative geometric volume: the exact lower endpoint.
- The existing volume/0.12 m recipe is evaluated separately using the pinned
  binary64 reference-depth value; all candidate recipe values remain unassigned.

The exact algebraic periodic-reference volume is also retained and checked
inside every propagated interval. The original integral, periodic-reference
value, interval and conservative lower endpoint are separate fields.

Area is recomputed from actual transformed triangle edges and actual transformed
endpoint displacements. For a source triangle, with `u=L(b-a)`, `v=L(c-a)` and
world L1 displacement bounds `da,db,dc`, set `alpha=da+db`,
`beta=da+dc`. Its seam-only area-change bound is:

`(alpha*|v|₁ + beta*|u|₁ + alpha*beta)/2`.

The nominal transformed triangle-area sum is enclosed with exact rational
square-root brackets on a 128-bit binary fraction grid. That is scalar evaluation
precision, not a geometry tolerance. Its evaluation interval width remains
separate from the seam-only bound. The reported intrinsic periodic-reference
area interval includes both. These are intrinsic geometric areas, not proof of
exposure, weather area, a primary wall face, joints or support.

## Conservative numeric representation and retained uncertainty

[Rounding review](../../../art/source/shared-map-fire-bindings/radial-measurement-certificates-v1/rounding-review.json)

selects their conservative lower quantity as the reference to represent;
multiple possible rounded outcomes across the full interval do not block

After the unchanged exact volume/reference-depth recipe, every certificate
computes the **largest finite nonnegative binary64 no greater than the exact
rational lower quantity**. For all 476 accepted-interpretation instances, that
source-side representation is certified. The 54 additional instances and eight
table unions carry conditional proposed representations.

Each selected or proposed source representation records:

- Its exact dyadic value, binary64 value, hexadecimal float and 64-bit pattern.
- The proof `dyadic <= exact lower < nextUp` and adjacent nonnegative bit patterns,
  which establishes maximality without rounding upward.
- Its nextUp value/bits/dyadic value and separate representation undermeasurement.
- A JSON scalar round-trip preserving the bits. Exact decimal-token containment
  in the candidate's nearest-even rounding cell independently certifies a
  correctly rounded binary64 parse.

Four scalar controls cover zero, an exact representable nonzero value, a
between-floats midpoint, and a between-floats value whose nearest-even conversion
rounds upward. Every control proves the floor value and nextUp inequality.
They are arithmetic controls, not zero-stock defaults or runtime tests.

All 530 full quantity intervals still span multiple nearest-even binary64
outcomes: 6–24 in the accepted cohort, 2–19 in the additional cohort.
Nearest-even conversion of the exact lower recipe quantity would round upward
for 492 instances, including 444 accepted ones. Those conversions are diagnostic
only; the selected/proposed representation always uses the proven lower value.

Source geometric intervals and conservative rational values remain intact.
The representation undermeasurement is recorded separately from the seam-only
bound. The scalar conversion is performed after the exact accepted recipe;

This is deterministic representation of the selected conservative reference,
