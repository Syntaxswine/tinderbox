## Findings that precede any correction

**No original parent record is omitted.** Every declared parent of every unrooted ID
appears in the diagnostic, including native hinges and leaf hardware. The difference
is reachability under proposed directions, classifications and contact search, rather
than lost records. Zero missing records is separate from a path to ground.

The proposed classifier treats `timber-fitting`, `rail` and opening frames as
dependents and verifies automatic contacts only between primary structural roles.
The original artist adjacency records use finite-area coplanar faces. Consequently,
actual embedded frame/cornice contacts and a shared-cut deck seam may never enter
that search. A coarse role also groups column bases, cornices, rails and ornaments;
promoting all `timber-fitting` objects would give decorative pieces unintended
bracing authority. The bounded proposal retains dependent roles and supplies exact
local relationships instead of changing that entire classification.

For example, `main/0/frieze-1/jamb--1` has no direct parent, although its actual solid
overlaps `main/0/cell-7-7/1` by 0.07 m in the minimum box projection. The same jamb
also overlaps the existing sill by approximately 0.069999531 m; the sill already has
wall support and lists the jamb as one of its own parents. This is a local frame
ownership issue, not evidence that glass should brace the frame or that all
attachment directions should reverse.

The fixed front header currently lists moving leaves as observed closed-pose bearing
parents. Native triangle contact with the measured left leaf is **true in both closed
and open poses**. Contact existence therefore does not establish intended permanent
fixed-frame support. The proposal uses the actual fixed jambs for that responsibility
and preserves the existing jamb-to-leaf hinges and leaf-to-hardware links.

## Real separation and useful alternative contacts

The main parapet and the clerestory rail require different decisions. The clerestory
rail already overlaps its rooted cornice; a named local joint addresses the missing
directed relationship without inventing a gap mount.

For `main-parapet-0/base`, actual separation from `roof/front-centre` is bounded below
by **0.246764736 m** and above by the recorded vertex-to-triangle witness distance
**0.309232877 m**. Its bounding boxes overlap, demonstrating why box overlap is not
contact. The same base is also separated from rooted `edge-0/cornice-7` by about
**0.21 m**. These are two exact candidate-parent measurements for the same base,
not two defects or a global nearest-parent claim. An authored structural/fastening
design remains open; no inferred mount grounds the 71-member component.

The sloped handrail's endpoint at `post--1-0` has a genuine **0.019074830–0.020029453 m**
separation interval. Its intermediate `post--1-10` nevertheless has actual volume
contact. The first control set uses that real contact. An endpoint gap alone does
not justify a new mount for the whole member.

The affected urns retain explicit actual cap/pier bearing records. Their upstream
piers/caps can be unrooted; that is not permission to attach the urns to remote
primary walls or the earth. Likewise, real native hinges and handles need their
fixed frame rooted, not independent anchors.

Projection and AABB distances are lower bounds; source vertex-to-triangle distances
are upper bounds. Triangle contact, positive volume overlap, mechanical fastening
and graph direction remain distinct findings. Bounds-ordered searches inspect only
three primary candidates per family representative; these are not nearest-solid
proofs or a complete stability analysis.
