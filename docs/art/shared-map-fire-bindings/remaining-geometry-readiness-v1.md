family counts and proof inputs. Approximate measurements are display values,
not assigned fuel. Source position/index/normal/UV arrays remain unchanged.

Predicates use source binary coordinates on an exact integer lattice and
rational affine terms. There is no approximate vertex weld, snap, contact
allowance or borrowed New York tolerance. Exact zero-area triangles are counted
and excluded only from integrals. Closed volume is certified only for a closed,
consistently oriented manifold with one surface component and either exact
convex supporting planes or congruent nonoverlapping prismatic caps.
Primary wall area additionally requires the actual native source front/depth
correspondence. Upward-area proof checks the highest plane and nonoverlapping
actual triangle projections. A bounding box never substitutes for a solid.

Literal small source seams and gaps remain findings, not repair orders.
Strict closure can remain unproved on a repeated radial primitive even when
its authoring intention is closed. No numerical policy is silently chosen to
make those cases pass.

## Compound leaves and furniture

[Compound review](../../../art/source/shared-map-fire-bindings/remaining-geometry-readiness-v1/compound-review.json)
covers all 53 accepted grouping intentions. Exact union volumes are proved for
45: eighteen leaves, eleven desks and sixteen benches. The eight cafe tables
remain withheld because their actual source component solids are not certified.
No envelope volume or nominal cylinder replaces that missing proof.

Desk intersections are subtracted using inclusion/exclusion only after the
actual components are certified full axis-aligned boxes. Disjoint source
components use their exact signed-solid measures. These are union measures,
not proof of a single connected solid, a joint or a support path.

All eighteen leaf area unions are proved separately. Their existing body and
extension keep the same relative offset in both declared source poses; this is
a metadata relationship check, not a posed sweep. Their actual local panel
area sum is about 2.7999999523 m², retaining the source Float32 representation.
The literal body/extension Y gap is about 2.384e-8 m and is reported rather than
filled. Hardware remains separately identified and inert. No hinge, matrix,
clearance or original geometry was changed.

## Glazing and ownership gaps

[Glazing review](../../../art/source/shared-map-fire-bindings/remaining-geometry-readiness-v1/glazing-ownership-review.json)
pins all 433 glass source records: 52 Administration and 381 Horticulture.
Every local-damage mapping remains unapproved. Source-prefix frame associations
are listed as discovery evidence, never as contact or support proof.

Twelve open-skin records have validated existing triangle-pair cell candidates:
3,072 cells on the great dome, 48 on its lantern, 18 on each cupola vent, and one
cell on each of eight large gallery sheets. These reference original index
intervals only. They are not a historical pane inventory, approved ownership or
an exported split. A gallery sheet containing only two huge triangles plainly
still needs reviewed local clipping/masking. An ordinary extruded glass record
also needs coherent front/back/edge retirement; deleting one triangle face is
not a pane break.

Do not convert a whole dome render ID into one rigid pane or first-hit whole-dome
retirement. Actual frame/patch correspondence and a bounded shared consumer
contract remain necessary. Inert material does not establish a brace, ground
anchor, invulnerability or a traversable water floor. The existing lagoon and
basin surfaces retain their accepted non-supporting semantics even if their
visual mesh happens to be closed.

Source ranges identify only their own triangles; merged render bounds are not
per-logical-part physical bounds. Compound intention, section membership,
touching decoration and common mesh hashes do not fill the remaining owner/
attachment gaps. No support edges, independent anchors or startup bindings are
created here.
