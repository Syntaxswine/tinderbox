# Source record for the Chicago city beat uniform

The outfit depicts a municipal street patrol in Chicago circa 1893. It draws the silhouette from a near-date photograph and the fabric color from a contemporary written description.

The University of Chicago finding aid lists the 1893 *Report of the General Superintendent of Police of the City of Chicago* (catalogue bib 383273). That physical volume was identified but not examined for this study. [University of Chicago finding aid](https://guides.lib.uchicago.edu/ChicagoStudies/PoliceandFD)

The exact 1893 season and issue details remain unverified. The forward helmet shell clears the native horns and ears. The collar cut, number of buttons, buckle, tablet marks, and black leather finish over the cloven hooves are art interpretations. All outfit surfaces are authored meshes and materials. The research images and publications stay out of the art package.
