# Siding smoke before ignition

As siding approaches ignition, a few warm-gray wisps drift up from the wall. They strengthen with the stored heat and disappear as it cools. When that site catches, its ordinary flame and smoke take over. This is presentation only: the effect adds no heat, light, embers, flame or charring.

The art candidate starts at published `7ba748719f848d3e41020d3f6e7d4ecbac378264`. [`siding-preheat.js`](../../../src/art/siding-preheat.js) reuses the pinned Ashland splat renderer. [`cottage-pair.js`](../../../src/art/cottage-pair.js) supplies the original Ashland wall site ID, local placement and outward normal. [`housing-flame-sites.js`](../../../src/art/housing-flame-sites.js) and [`neighborhood.js`](../../../src/art/neighborhood.js) project ordinary catalogue heat onto the nearest existing exposed siding anchor in the same intact section, within 1.35 m. Door openings, joined faces, removed sections and interior samples without such an anchor stay quiet. Houses that fade to keep the player visible also suppress this cue. [`index.js`](../../../src/art/index.js) gives both paths one depth-tested batch and keeps the ordinary fire batches unchanged.

Opacity eases in above heat fraction **0.43**, is raised gently through the midrange, and peaks at **0.98**. Peak alpha is **0.30** on the attached wisp and **0.24** on each of two tapered rising lobes. Stem sigma is 0.063–0.102 m wide, 0.092–0.163 m high and 0.031–0.066 m deep; lobe sigma spans about 0.056–0.141, 0.074–0.150 and 0.036–0.091 m respectively. The wisps drift on the world clock with the existing wind where available, and their centers remain outside the wall. No smoke timer continues after the heat drops below the onset. One shared batch reserves at most **12 sites / 36 splats**; it draws before normal burning effects and cannot displace them. A missing additive field yields no warning.
