# Carried torch light

Artist candidate from published `801807b2e71638101861c875c2d23c9ad73ad17b`. The equipped torch illuminates the path and nearby siding as soon as the player presses **Light**. A separate **Ignite** press remains the only torch action that applies heat. No simulation, inventory, tool duration, exposure, camera, vendor assets or ignition behavior changed.

[`src/art/carried-torch.js`](../../../src/art/carried-torch.js) uses one warm spotlight with soft edges and a 3.8 m cutoff. It follows the true torch attachment and shares the flame's world clock, with 4.5% flicker. The existing night scene already renders shadows; the torch uses one 256 × 256 shadow view. At the spawn pose the native tip extends slightly through the Ashland siding. A short check against the existing solid geometry pulls only the light back onto the holder's side, preserving the flame and the held asset. The light retires with the flame when holstered, spent, reset or disposed. There is no new heat or light state in simulation.

## WebGL review

Opened the production package through **Play → Catalogue Street** at `?cottages=1`. Pressing Light changed the HUD to Ignite with no burning ground cells. The [browser receipt](browser-receipt.json) and the JPGs then captured the same packaged scene using its normal camera, default zoom and exposure, on Three r180 / ANGLE D3D11 / GeForce RTX 3080. The review page invoked the real game composition with controlled existing inputs, at a 1,280 × 414 canvas. It is review-only and is not included in the package.

- [Unlit](previews/01-unlit.jpg) and [lit](previews/02-lit.jpg): identical projection, view matrix and exposure. At the same pose, the facing siding and near paving gained warm light; the far paving probe beyond the cutoff was unchanged. All 12 matches remained, with no burning ground or cottage cells and no Ignite press.
- [After walking](previews/03-walking-end.jpg) and [turned toward the wall](previews/04-turned-to-wall.jpg): the player traveled 3.30 m, and the native torch tip moved 4.70 m including the turn along the route. Light followed the attachment. At the second pose the [holstered view](previews/06-holstered.jpg) has the identical camera and exposure, with the pool gone while the underlying torch timer remained lit. Reset returned the effect to zero intensity.
- [Spawn shadow-off control](previews/02b-wall-shadow-off-control.jpg) and [walked shadow-off control](previews/05-wall-shadow-off-control.jpg) deliberately disable only the torch shadow for comparison. They show a local difference, with 433 and 67 pixels respectively differing by more than 12 RGB levels in at least one channel. In the shadowed candidate the light stays on the near side at the blocked pose; the distant pavement probe reads the same RGB with and without the torch. This review covers these visible street surfaces. Wall cutaways continue to use the scene's existing shadow rules.
