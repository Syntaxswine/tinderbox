## Accepted adaptations and boundaries

Accept the exact A01–A15 and H01–H22 family scopes, existing profile choices and
quantity recipes in the proposal, **except H15's 412 living plant parts remain
withheld**. These are game material assignments, not assertions about concealed
historical construction. No colour or native authoring token assigns physics.
The contemporary Iron Age [World's Fair report](https://americanprotectivetariffleague.org/library/1892-anon-iron-age-1892-09-01-vol-50/)
does identify ornamental ironwork at Horticulture's dome; extending metal to all
modelled framing remains the stated game adaptation.

Timber uses the existing physical timber semantics and the explicitly selected
clapboard/partition/frame/door/roof/furniture profile. Glass and metal use their
existing inert boundary semantics. Masonry, mineral trim, quay and soil proxies
need explicit mappings to existing nonfuel semantics, preserving their source
identity and intended support role. A nonfuel choice neither anchors an object
nor makes it indestructible or a brace.

The 18 paired leaves retain their original bodies/extensions and one account per
leaf; inert hardware and real hinge transforms remain separate. The eleven desks,
eight tables and sixteen benches are accepted grouping intentions. Closed union
volume, intersections and actual component ownership still require proof. A shared
mesh/batch never combines unrelated furniture or duplicates its fuel.

All area/volume recipes require actual effective triangles at native dimensions.
Preserve source ranges, holes, original UVs, thickness and offsets. Withhold an
unproved closed-volume quantity and return its exact geometry exception. Do not
copy New York cap repairs, joins, coatings, numerical tolerances or anchors. No
new lacquer account is accepted.

Glazing keeps local damage/retirement ownership. A dome-wide render mesh is not
one rigid pane or an instruction to remove the dome at the first damaged patch.
Use actual frame/pane structure and the shared local model; any derived split
must preserve original geometry and be reviewed explicitly.

Lagoon/basin water remains nonfuel and non-supporting; preserve dry-ground
navigation and the actual separate visual surfaces. Do not introduce a solid
walkable water floor, fuel, suppression/flow system or unmeasured heat capacity.
Thermal/picking representation and any oil-on-inert-surface interaction need
ordinary-tool controls in the common adapter, without seeding hidden fuel through
an inert receiver. No map-specific tool callback implements a water rule.
