## Bounded implementation and strict embeds

Prefer actual closed native-fragment geometry where it establishes the common
material. The 630 pairs without crossing outer facets also have strict original
solid overlap; facet-query false is not evidence of a gap.

The existing surface-based kernel does not always define a closed material volume
for an open fitting facet. Do not add a general volume simulation to resolve this.
For an intended fixed embed, prototype an explicit conservative assembly bond:

- Bind each endpoint once to an exact original native fragment of its named source
  part and connected component. Record its original facet/cell identity, effective
  geometry hashes and local selector.
- Prove the original strict embed witness and a bounded continuous material path
  from that witness to its original fragment port, within the same closed source
  component. A local selector alone or an open facet cannot prove a volume.
- Keep the original owners after damage. The bond exists only while both assigned
  ports remain intact and in the permitted assembly pose. Remove that bond when an
  owner retires; never select another surviving or remote face. Other independently
  proved bonds and native material continuity retain their existing meaning.
- Label this as an architect-selected assembly-ownership approximation. It may
  conservatively sever a bond before all historical interior material is consumed;
  it is not a claim that an intact old mesh proves present surviving volume.

Port selection must be deterministic, local and derived from actual geometry.
Report distances, tie handling, disconnected/cut cases and unresolved mappings.
There is no arbitrary distance tolerance, filled voxel, new support lattice,
thermal cell, fuel account, any/all surface-survival inference or whole-carrier
retirement rule. Existing named mechanical fastenings keep their own semantics.

Coder may prototype this adapter on a representative strict embed and a multi-region
cornice pair, with closed-fragment, real gap, disconnected/void, original-port loss,
no-remap and cold/active controls. Return exact source/evidence and remaining
exceptions before installing the full family table. All earlier ten-control,
quantity, portal and local-retirement controls remain mandatory. This is not
authorization to weaken a source check or silently implement a different solver.
